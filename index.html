ABOUT TEENS SUMMIT GLOBAL

Raising a Generation of Purpose-Driven Teen Leaders

Teens Summit Global is committed to empowering teenagers through leadership development, mentorship, education, character formation and transformational experiences.

We believe every young person has the potential to influence their world positively when given the right guidance, opportunities and support.

Our commitment is to inspire, educate and equip teenagers to become responsible, principled and impactful leaders.
