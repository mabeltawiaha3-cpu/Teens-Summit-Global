Who We Are

About Teens Summit Global

Teens Summit Global (TSG) is a youth-focused initiative committed to helping teenagers become purposeful, responsible, confident and transformational leaders.

Through practical workshops, mentorship, leadership experiences and school-based programmes, we equip young people with the mindset and tools to make meaningful contributions to society.

Our Mandate

Vision & Mission

A clear purpose. A generation equipped to make a difference.

Our Vision

To raise a generation of purpose-driven, transformational teen leaders who positively influence their schools, communities and the world.

Our Mission

To inspire, educate and equip teenagers through leadership development, mentorship, values-based learning and transformative experiences.

Core Objectives & Values

Purpose • Integrity • Excellence • Service • Responsibility • Respect • Innovation • Empowerment.

Our SDG Commitment

We support inclusive and equitable quality education (SDG 4) and gender equality and empowerment (SDG 5), creating opportunities for every teen to grow and lead.
